# Split-screen co-op

Two players in one game on one computer, each with their own camera and half of the screen.
It is on in every game: a second player joins whenever they like and leaves whenever they like, and the screen splits and goes back to one. `SMS_COOP=0` turns it off; an online game (`SMS_MULTIPLAYER`) never has it. (Before, the launcher's **Play co-op** turned it on with `SMS_COOP=1`, and plain Play never took a second player.)

- The first player plays as ever, on the keyboard and mouse or a controller.
- A second player joins by pressing **Start on another controller** (pressed and let go) while a level is played, or, pressed during a load, a cutscene or the pause menu, as soon as play starts ("Player 2 is ready"): they appear beside Mario as Luigi (Mario's model in Luigi's green), with FLUDD, and the screen splits in two.
- Or as the character the launcher's Character page chose for them (an outfit picked for player 2): `SMS_COOP_CHARACTER=<id>` is `mario` or a character in `mods/characters` (as `SMS_CHARACTER` names one), and for Mario `SMS_COOP_LOOK` is their outfit's colours (`hat=2fa846;shirt=...`, as multiplayer sends a player's), painted from the launcher's `SMS_MARIO_PAINT`; with no colours they are Mario as on the disc. Either way never in the first player's colours (the launcher's `SMS_MARIO_LOOK` pack). The notices name them ("Princess Peach joined!"). They keep their character's model, animations, look and FLUDD size and place, but speak with the first player's character's voice: the game has one voice bank.
  Player 2 is Luigi as ever, and the log says why, when the character isn't in `mods/characters`, when the game plays as a code mod's own characters (Super Mario Eclipse), or in a stage whose memory has no room for a second character's archive beside the first's.
  Holding Start for 1.5 seconds leaves (a bar fills while they hold it), and the screen goes back to one; a short press of their Start is the pause menu, as the first player's is. Unplugging their controller keeps their place for 10 seconds (they stand still, a bar counts down in their view): the controller plugged in again, or any other controller pressed meanwhile, takes it; else they leave.
- What to press is on a card near the top of the picture (`platform/gx/src/gx_coop_overlay.cpp`), or while the screen is split in the view of the player it is about, with a badge in that player's colour (player 1 red, player 2 green), and the controller's Start as it is printed (START, OPTIONS, +, MENU). It names players by number, not character: either may play as anyone (the Characters page, F4). Its text is Inter, the launcher's typeface (`third_party/inter_ascii.h`, an ASCII cut under the SIL Open Font License), drawn with stb_truetype. Nothing nags a player alone: the card shows only when another controller is used, and for what just happened (joined, left, disconnected).
- Whose controller is whose. The first player has the keyboard and mouse and at most one controller: the first one used, in the menus or a level, while the keyboard has not been used yet. Once the keyboard has, it is the first player's device and controllers are guests, so one player on the keyboard and mouse and one on a controller works whoever touched what first. Every other controller is a guest: in the menus it plays as the first player's too, so anyone can pick a file, but in a level it moves no one until it joins, so someone picking up a second controller never moves Mario. Anything they press shows the card: "Join as Player 2: Press START to join".
  Holding a guest's Start for 1.5 seconds makes it the first player's controller instead (they moved to it); the one they had becomes a guest. The held Start is no pause. A queued join is taken back the same way.
  A controller that took the first player before the keyboard was used joins too: holding its Start for 1.5 seconds in a level, once the keyboard has been used, gives the first player to the keyboard and joins as the second (a short press is the pause menu, on its release). The card says so when both are in use together, at most once a minute.
  The first player's controller unplugged, the next controller used is theirs (the keyboard still works meanwhile).
  A controller seen twice (DS4Windows, BetterJoy or Steam Input beside the controller itself) presses the same buttons on both at once: the second is the first's twin, plays as it and never joins. The log names each controller as it connects.
  A GameCube adapter's ports are controllers of their own, the same way.
- A level makes player 2 as it loads, only when a controller other than the first player's is there, or a controller and a keyboard in use (or one has joined or asked to): a player alone on a controller plays every level as before. A controller connected in the middle of a level joins from the next area (the card says so). So does one in a level where the first player switched characters (F4), which is for a player alone only while nobody has joined.
- Whatever either player collects counts for both, as in multiplayer: coins (every 50 a life for both), red and blue coins, 1-Ups, the 100-coin Shine and Shines are the game's own counts. A Shine the second player touches is theirs: the Shine Get plays full screen around them, and the save prompt follows. A Shine that keeps the players in the stage (Super Mario Eclipse's) lets them play on after it, as it does Mario. Hearts, fruit and water bottles fill the health or FLUDD of whoever took them, and a nozzle box gives its nozzle to their FLUDD. Lives are shared too: the second player losing a life (an enemy, a pit) takes one from the shared count, the HUD's, which shows it, and they are back beside Mario three seconds later. Lost on the last one (×00), the death plays and they leave the screen: Mario carries on alone, and the next life that comes (a 1-Up, 50 coins) is theirs and brings them back. Mario losing his last life is the game over as ever. (Falling out of the world, or being left far behind, puts them back beside Mario at once, with no life lost.)
- Enemies go after whoever is nearer: each object that moves near the second player moves as if they were Mario (`objectMove`). Either player can talk to people and read signs, take pipes and paintings (everyone goes), and get Shines. A talk stops only the player talking: their camera goes in, their A and B read it, and the other player plays on. If they leave in the middle, the first player reads the rest. A doorway that takes a player into a building or out of it (Delfino Plaza's) takes the other along too; put beside them on the doorway's way back, they stay until they step off it and onto it again.
- A Puffer (Super Mario Eclipse's POINK) sticks to either player's FLUDD, one on each nozzle at a time: the second player pumps theirs up with their R and shoots it off their nozzle. One stuck on the second player when they lose a life or leave bursts.
- On Pinna Park's roller coaster (Mecha-Bowser's ride, and the coaster's balloons) the second player rides a car of their own, a car's length behind Mario's, and turns and sprays from it with their controller, on their own camera, as Mario does from his.
- The screen is split while both play the level. Cutscenes, a Shine Get and other moments the game shows on its own camera are full screen, on the first player's camera, as is everything while the second player is out.
- Each player has their own health meter and FLUDD gauge (water and nozzle), in their own view: side by side, at the right of each view, the game's size (the health meter a little lower, clear of the lives); one above the other, at half size at the right of each view. What both share (Shines, blue coins, coins, lives, messages) is drawn once, where the game has it.
- Sounds are heard from whichever player's camera is nearer: the second player's FLUDD, footsteps and the enemies around them are heard however far they are from Mario.
- `SMS_COOP_SPLIT=stacked` puts the views one above the other (the first player's on top); by default they are side by side (the first player's on the left). The launcher's Settings → Display has it as **Co-op split screen**.
- Save states are off while two play. Co-op is off in multiplayer games (`SMS_MULTIPLAYER`).

## How it works

[`game/coop.cpp`](game/coop.cpp), with the input in [`platform/pad/pad.cpp`](../../platform/pad/pad.cpp) and the views in [`platform/gx`](../../platform/gx/src/gx_render.cpp).

**Controller 2.** The port used to put every controller into controller 1. It now keeps each SDL controller's state apart (by its instance id), puts together all but the second player's for controller 1, and gives the second player's its own `PADStatus` on channel 1, which the game reads as it reads channel 1 on a GameCube. `port_coop_*` tell the game who joined and left. Mouse look stays the first player's camera's (`port_camera_take_mouse` gives the second camera nothing), and so does what it leaves behind: the camera's tilt (the mouse's, or the C-stick orbit's), the mouse wheel's zoom and the closer following while the mouse moves are kept per player and swapped with the other globals, so player 1's mouse no longer tilts, zooms or tightens camera 2 (player 2's camera keeps the game's distance).

**Player 2** is a second `TMario` (`TCoopMario`), made from the stage's own Mario record (the one the loader read, kept by `zzzzz-ext-20`) while the stage loads (the `sceneLoaded` hook, before the stage lets its parameter files go), so they have Mario's parameters, models and FLUDD. They go in the player group (`プレーヤーグループ`), beside Mario, so the stage moves, animates, hits and draws them with him; until someone joins they do nothing and touch nothing. The game already runs other `TMario`s than Mario (Shadow Mario, Il Piantissimo): the Mario code works on `this`, and reaches "the player" through `gpMarioOriginal`, `gpMarioAddress` and the access globals (`gpMarioPos`, `SMS_SetMarioAccessParams`). While player 2 runs (their move, their camera's, their view's drawing), those globals are theirs, and so are `gpCamera` and the camera's helpers (`gpCameraShake`, `gpCameraMario`, `gpCameraOption`), so the game's own code plays them as it plays Mario. Both players' surroundings are in play, as each player's own game is in multiplayer: the stage's actors are clipped against both cameras in the stage's update, not only while drawing (`port_coop_views`; an actor only player 2 saw was clipped out, so it neither moved nor drew in their view, and a map object's model was hidden), and each player has their own area and fast cubes (`TMario::thinkCube` sets them for whoever is `gpMarioOriginal`, so player 2's moves used to overwrite Mario's), a place in either player's being in play (`port_coop_other_cube`, patch `zzzzzz-coop-03`). Things that are the whole stage's stay the first player's: a fall into a pit is theirs alone (the director's hell-dead flag is put back).

**Doorways.** Player 2 follows a warp of Mario's (and Mario theirs) to a place beside him, a little behind and to his right (`placeBeside`). Delfino Plaza's buildings are entered and left by warp floors (`TMapWarp::watchToWarp`, which also wakes the map model of the place it takes you to and puts the others to sleep), and each puts you a step from the other: the player put beside the one who went through stood on the way back and went straight back through, and the other after them, each warp parting the players or waking the model of the place the other was not in (the camera then showed the sky, or Mario was put up at the interior's height). A warp floor under a player `placeBeside` put somewhere is now held (`port_coop_warp_held`, patch `zzzzzz-coop-13`, asked in each player's pass of `watchToWarp`) until they step off it; the spots beside them are no help there, as the doorways are narrow and their walls push a player put beside back onto the warp floor.

**A talk is its talker's.** The game has one player: a talk stops Mario through his controller (flag 8 turns its stick and buttons into the talk's), `TMario::playerControl` turns every `TMario` to the person while a talk starts, its script reads `gpMarioOriginal`, and `TTalk2D2` reads the first controller and moves `gpCamera`. Patch `zzzzzz-coop-15` makes all of that follow the player whose press started it (`port_coop_talk_taken`; a talk any other way is Mario's). Flag 8 goes on the talker's controller alone, and is not copied to the other's as the rest of the flags are (`port_coop_talk_pad`). The talk's script and window run with their globals (`port_coop_enter_talker`), so it is they who turn to the person and their camera that goes in. Whoever is not talking is not turned and can spray (`port_coop_free_in_talk`), in a world the talk has paused as it always does. Player 2 leaving during theirs hands it to Mario (`talkerLeft`): his controller reads it and his camera goes in, or the stage would wait for them for ever. The talk's window is still drawn at the right of the whole frame, so on side by side views it sits in player 2's half whoever talks.

**Carrying each other.** A player can pick the other up (B, facing them): `TMario` can take an actor of its own type, `0x80000001`. The carried player is put in the carrier's right hand every frame, and `hitMario` keeps them from bumping, but `checkCollision` called `keepDistance` a second time after it: the jump's pose brings the hand inside the bump's radius, and each frame in the air pushed the carrier off their own hand, sideways. Patch `zzzzzz-coop-14` drops that second call while one of the two players carries the other (`port_coop_carrying`); anything else is bumped as it was.

**Puffers on a nozzle.** A Puffer (`TPopo`) that catches a player sticks to "Mario's" nozzle and is moved in the stage's update, which `objectMove` plays with player 2 when they are the one it caught; but it is put on the nozzle in the animation pass (`TPopo::calcRootMatrix`, reading `SMS_GetMarioWaterGun()`), which always ran with Mario's globals, so one that caught player 2 sat on Mario's FLUDD, and the stage's one nozzle flag (`TPopoManager::mIsNozzleFree`) let only one player have one. Patch `zzzzzz-coop-11` tells `coop.cpp` whose nozzle a Puffer takes and leaves (`port_coop_popo_grab`); it then moves with that player (`playerFor`), `calcRootMatrix` runs as player 2 for one on theirs (`port_coop_enter_popo_player`), and while two play the nozzle flag is asked per player (`port_coop_popo_nozzle_free`). One on player 2 when they are down or out of play is killed (as getting on Yoshi kills Mario's).

**The roller coaster.** Pinna Park's coaster stages (map 58) start Mario in his car (`TMario::isUnUsualStageStart`, `toroccoStart`: `MARIO_STATUS_TOROCCO`, in which `calcBaseMtx` puts him where the track's animation has the car). Every `TMario` made there loads a car and its own copy of the track's animation (`initModel`), player 2's too, but only Mario was ever put in his. `rideCoaster` puts player 2 in theirs (and out of it when Mario is out of his) and plays their track at Mario's frame less the frames a car's length takes at the car's speed now (kept to that length as the speed changes, and from the track's end once Mario has been round). Their camera takes the coaster camera as Mario's does, from their status, and the warp follows are off while either rides (a car round the track's end is no warp).

**Two ears.** JAudio places each sound effect and sequence in camera 1's view every frame (`JAIBasic::checkNextFrameSe`, `checkPlayingSeq`), and that place is its volume, pan, surround, priority and whether it is dropped as too far (beyond 5000 units): a sound by player 2 far from Mario went unheard. While player 2 plays, the place is camera 2's instead when that camera is nearer (`port_coop_hear`, patch `zzzzzz-coop-08`; a sound moving to the other camera gets no Doppler jump), `MSound::getDistFromCamera` is the nearer camera's (the water hitting the ground, the stage's ambient sounds), and where a stage hears only the room its listener is in (Delfino Hotel), a room either player is in is heard. The game is set up for one audio camera (`audioCameraMax`); JAudio's own several cameras would have panned each camera's sounds hard to one side.

**Each player's HUD.** Player 2 has a HUD of their own: a second `TGCConsole2`, made in `stageSetup` from the stage's (its scene record), its `loadAfter` with player 2's globals. The game's HUD's perform (`port_coop_hud`, patch `zzzzzz-coop-09`) updates it right after the game's, with player 2's globals, so its health meter and FLUDD gauge are theirs as the game's are Mario's; of the sounds it makes, only its health's and FLUDD's are played (the counters' are the game's HUD's already), and its counters make no sparkles. While the screen is split, the HUD is drawn a part at a time (`port_hud_set_drawn`, `ext/core/game/port_hud.cpp`, which hides the other parts for a draw as the HUD settings do): the shared parts where the game has them, then each player's meters moved and scaled into their view (`GXPC_SetSplitView`; its scissor keeps them in it). A draw of the meters alone leaves out every other group of the layout (a code mod's own too, as Eclipse's Shadow Mario brush card, which Eclipse hides on the game's HUD only) and runs no code mod's draw callbacks (BetterSunshineEngine's) and no radar. Player 2's gauge is put back down as they come back from a lost life: while their death plays, their HUD takes their camera's death close-up for a cutscene and hides the gauge (Mario's death reloads the stage, so the game's never needs this).

**Player 2's character** ([`game/coop_character.cpp`](game/coop_character.cpp)). Player 2 is made from the `"mario"` volume, as Mario is (`TMario::load`, `loadAfter`), and it holds the first player's character's archive. For another character, theirs is read into the stage's heap once a stage (`ApplicationCharacterArchive`, patch `zzzzzz-character-06`; the disc's own for Mario) and put on the volume around player 2's `load` and `loadAfter` only, then the first player's back (`ApplicationCharacterMounted`, `ApplicationCharacterPut`, patch [`zzzzzz-coop-12`](../../decomp-patches/zzzzzz-coop-12-player2-character.patch)), so the rest of the stage reads the first player's. The same patch keeps a character's look model (`look/default.bmd`, patch `zzzzzz-character-04`) and FLUDD place (`character/fludd.bin`, patch `zzzzzz-character-05`) per player, by whether player 2's globals were in force as the model was made (`port_coop_in_p2`): there was one of each, the last `TMario` made's, so player 2 took the first player's. A chosen Mario's body, hands and cap get textures of their own, as Luigi's do (below), each then given its picture in their colours (`mpg_dress_look`, [`mp_look.cpp`](../multiplayer/sync/mp_look.cpp)) or kept plain (`mpg_dress_plain`), out of the first player's look pack; FLUDD's are the first player's.

**Luigi's colours.** Mario's models share their textures between every load of them, so player 2's body, hands and cap get texture tables of their own (each a block of headers, images and palettes, its offsets positive for the 64-bit build), and the body's main texture is recoloured as multiplayer's Luigi is ([`mp_recolour.h`](../multiplayer/mp_recolour.h)). The body's display lists are made in `loadAfter`, after that; the cap's are made again.

**Camera 2** is a second `CPolarSubCamera` (`TCoopCamera`), made while the stage loads, reading controller 2, following player 2 (it moves right after them). Camera 2 gets its own list of the actors the close-up view can turn to (`setNoticeInfo`, as the director makes camera 1's), or Y read an unmade list. A controller's meanings (`TMarioGamePad::updateMeaning`) follow the camera of the player holding it (`port_coop_pad_camera`, patch `zzzzzz-coop-02`): controller 2 reads as in the close-up view Y opens while camera 2 is in it, not camera 1.

**Two views.** The `drawStage` hook draws the stage itself while both play: the frame's set-up and the goop's textures once; then each view into its part of the frame (`GXPC_SetSplitView`: viewports, scissors, EFB copies and their clears and peeks are moved and scaled into it after the widescreen mapping), with the stage's GX lists, the silhouettes and the 3D part of the GX Post list; then the 2D (the HUD, the screen's effects, the frame's copy) once over both, and a line between the views. The first view is drawn from the entry pass the game makes after each tick, as when alone. For the second, the entry pass (each model's view matrices, the draw buffers) is made again with camera 2 in camera 1's place in the lists; goop is stamped from the first only (`TPollutionManager::stampModel`). The game's view-frustum check (`ViewFrustumClipCheck`) counts what either camera sees as in view, so what either player sees moves and draws in both. Each camera's aspect is its part's (half or twice the game's) while the screen is split.

Patches: [`zzzzzz-coop-01-split-screen`](../../decomp-patches/zzzzzz-coop-01-split-screen.patch) (the hooks, the frustum check, the goop stamp), [`zzzzzz-coop-08-player2-heard`](../../decomp-patches/zzzzzz-coop-08-player2-heard.patch) (the second ear; player 2, camera 2 and their HUD forgotten as the stage goes), [`zzzzzz-coop-09-player2-hud`](../../decomp-patches/zzzzzz-coop-09-player2-hud.patch) (the HUD's perform), [`zzzzzz-coop-10-player2-shine-get-end`](../../decomp-patches/zzzzzz-coop-10-player2-shine-get-end.patch) (a Shine Get that keeps the players in the stage ends for whoever took the Shine: BetterSunshineEngine puts `gpMarioAddress` back in play, so player 2 is it while they are in the Shine Get, `port_coop_shine_get_player`).

## Testing

Headless, with scripted input (`SMS_AUTOPRESS` for player 1) and these test aids:

| Variable | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `SMS_COOP_CHARACTER=id`, `SMS_COOP_LOOK=look` | player 2's character and (for `mario`) colours, as the launcher sets them; `SMS_MARIO_PAINT` is the launcher's paint folder |
| `SMS_COOP_TEST_JOIN=n` | a second player without a controller joins `n` frames after the stage starts playing |
| `SMS_COOP_TEST_PAD=x:y@frame+frames,a@frame+frames,...` | that player's stick (-100..100), or a button (`a@`, `b@`, `x@`, `y@`, `z@`, `l@`, `r@`), frames since they joined |
| `SMS_AUTOCONTROLLER=id:button@field+fields,id:axis=value@field+fields,id:gone@field,...` | (platform/pad) a pretend SDL controller's buttons and axes, by SDL number (A 0, B 1, X 2, Y 3, Start 6; axes 0-5), through the same path as a real one: its Start joins as player 2; `gone` unplugs it. Its first event connects it: send one early (a small axis value) so the level makes player 2 |
| `SMS_COOP_TEST_LEAVE=n` | they leave `n` frames after joining |
| `SMS_COOP_TEST_KILL=frame` | they lose a life (frames since the game started) |
| `SMS_COOP_TEST_LIVES=n` | the shared lives are set to `n` as they join |
| `SMS_COOP_TEST_ONE_UP=n` | a 1-Up `n` frames after they joined |
| `SMS_COOP_TEST_COINS=n:count` | from `n` frames after they joined, `count` coins brought out at them, one each 10 frames, for the item group's hit check to take |
| `SMS_COOP_TEST_HURT=n` | Mario loses 2 health and player 2 loses 3, and player 2's FLUDD is left a third full, `n` frames after they joined (each one's meters in their own view) |
| `SMS_COOP_TEST_FLUDD=n` | FLUDD put on their back `n` frames after they joined (as a stage that starts without it hands it out) |
| `SMS_COOP_TEST_SHINE=n` | the stage's 100-coin Shine brought out `n` frames after they joined, and player 2 put on it 360 frames later (the item group's hit check gives it to them) |
| `SMS_COOP_TEST_WARP=frame:area,episode` | the stage changes |
| `SMS_COOP_TEST_AT=x,y,z,angle` | player 2 put there, facing `angle` (0-65535), 10 frames after joining, their camera behind them |
| `SMS_COOP_TEST_PAD1=...` | as `SMS_COOP_TEST_PAD`, on controller 1, while player 2 plays |
| `SMS_COOP_TEST_FACE=n` | player 2 stood `n` units in front of player 1, facing them, 10 frames after joining |
| `SMS_COOP_TEST_NPC=1\|2` | that player stood a step from the nearest person, facing them, 10 frames after joining |
| `SMS_COOP_TRACE=1` | logs each player's every change of status, with where they are and how fast they go, and the stage's talks: whose, who to, the controllers' flags, which camera is in |
| `SMS_COOP_TEST_POPO=n[,1]` | a Puffer brought out in front of player 2 `n` frames after they joined, and with `,1` one in front of Mario 5 frames later (a stage with Puffers: Bianco Hills' 4th episode, `SMS_WARP=2,3`) |
| `SMS_COOP_DEBUG=1` | the stage's lists as found, and both players' places once a second |
